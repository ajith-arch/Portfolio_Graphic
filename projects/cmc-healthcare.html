Info

Project overview

Challenge

CMC Vellore needed a clearer digital experience that could help patients, students, researchers, and visitors quickly find care, appointments, departments, education, and hospital information.

Approach

I redesigned the experience around patient-first navigation, quick access cards, appointment booking, department discovery, education and research storytelling, and a calm trustworthy healthcare visual system.

Outcome

A polished responsive healthcare website concept with stronger hierarchy, improved service discovery, clearer appointment pathways, and a scalable content structure for patient care, education, and research.

Details

Design direction

  • Calm medical blue visual system
  • Clean white space
  • Soft rounded cards
  • Trustworthy healthcare typography
  • Quick-access service cards
  • Appointment-focused CTAs
  • Department and care journey sections
  • Education and research storytelling
  • Patient support and FAQ visibility

Designed to make healthcare access feel clearer, calmer, and more trustworthy.

The redesign brings together patient care, appointments, departments, education, research, and hospital information into one clean digital experience built for clarity, trust, and accessibility.
