Info

Project overview

Challenge

The existing campus retail experience needed a more modern ecommerce flow that could support students, alumni, and visitors with clearer product discovery and stronger campus branding.

Approach

I redesigned the experience around category storytelling, large lifestyle visuals, clear shopping paths, and focused product sections for Women, Men, Kids, Clearance, and store information.

Outcome

A polished responsive web experience with improved hierarchy, stronger merchandising zones, clearer navigation, and a scalable visual system for future campus campaigns.

Design direction

Visual system

  • Bold athletic typography
  • Full-bleed video / image hero sections
  • Color-coded category navigation
  • Large editorial product sections
  • Clean retail product cards
  • High-contrast CTA buttons
  • Campus identity through red, teal, navy, orange & yellow accents
