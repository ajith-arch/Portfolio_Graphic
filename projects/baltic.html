Info

Project overview

Challenge

Baltic and Spaceone needed a digital experience that could tell the story of an unlikely collaboration — heritage watchmaking meeting deconstructed, forward-looking design.

Approach

I built an editorial microsite around the Seconde Majeure — scroll-driven reveals, craftsmanship storytelling, collection browsing, and a dark luxury aesthetic rooted in horology.

Outcome

A polished responsive site showcasing the watch's kinematic dial, Charbonné finishing, manufacture movement details, and the four-piece collection with preorder CTAs.

Details

Design direction

  • Dark editorial luxury aesthetic
  • Serif display typography for heritage tone
  • Scroll-to-reveal product storytelling
  • Charbonné & brushed dial finish showcases
  • Craftsmanship macro photography sections
  • Collection grid with spec-driven cards
  • Preorder-focused conversion flow

Precision beyond motion — crafted for collectors who read time differently.

The site brings together collaboration narrative, product craft, and collection discovery into one immersive digital experience built for luxury watch enthusiasts.
